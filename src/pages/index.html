FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to know how to run ads?
No. The launcher takes you through four steps and the AI writes the ads from your website. If you can post on Facebook, you can do this.
Is there really no setup fee?
Yes. It's $297 a month. You sign up, get instant access and go straight into the ad launcher.
What do I pay for the ads themselves?
Your ad budget goes directly to Meta from your own ad account. You choose the daily amount and can change or pause it any time. Texts, calls and emails sent from the tool are billed by usage.
Do you run my ads for me?
No, and that's the point. We're not an agency. You run your own ads with a tool that makes it simple, and we show you what's working on the live webinar every week.
How is this different from signing up to HighLevel directly?
Selling with Social is powered by HighLevel, so you get the same platform. On top you get our ad launcher, AI ads written from your website, video scripts and ideas, and live training every week.
Can I cancel?
Yes. It's month to month.
Will it work for my kind of business?
If your customers are on Facebook or Instagram, yes. Owners use it for trades, local services, professional firms, restaurants, coaching and online stores.
